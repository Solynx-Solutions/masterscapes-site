FOUNDER & CEO

MEET DENNIS HERNANDEZ

FOUNDER & CEO | MASTER CRAFTSMAN

Dennis Hernandez has always believed that mastery is earned through discipline and consistency. As a Black Belt in Tae Kwon Do and former owner of MVP Tae Kwon Do, Dennis spent years teaching students the importance of focus, intent, and perseverance. Those same principles now guide his work in transforming landscapes and designing pools.

With hands-on experience in pavers, outdoor living spaces, and custom designs, Dennis approaches every project with efficiency and craftsmanship. For him, landscaping is more than construction—it’s an art form where precision meets creativity, and every detail reflects the client’s lifestyle.

At Master Scapes, Dennis calls this the Pinnacle of Craftsmanship. Because when discipline meets design, the result is not just a yard—it’s a place where families gather, memories are made, and Lifestyles are elevated.
